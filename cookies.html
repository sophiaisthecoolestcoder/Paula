Cookies & Local Storage Cookies & lokale Speicherung

This website uses no cookies at all. It stores just two small values on your device — the language you choose, and the fact that you dismissed the notice — and neither is written unless you act.

In short

  • No cookies. This site sets none, and no third party sets any through it.
  • No tracking or analytics. No visitor statistics, no advertising, no profiling, no fingerprinting.
  • No third-party content. Fonts, images and the background video are served from this site's own server. There are no embedded videos, maps, social media plugins or web fonts loaded from other providers.
  • No consent banner, because there is nothing here that requires your consent. See why below.

What is stored on your device

Two entries, both in your browser's local storage:

NameTypePurposeContentDuration
lang Local storage Remembers whether you are reading the site in English or German, so you do not have to switch again on every page. The text en or de. Nothing else — no identifier, no number, nothing that could identify you. Until you clear your browser's site data.
noticeSeen Local storage Remembers that you closed the short notice about cookies, so it is not shown to you again. The value 1. Nothing else. Until you clear your browser's site data.

Neither value is written when you simply open the site. The language entry is written only at the moment you click EN or DE; the notice entry only when you dismiss the notice. If you do neither, nothing is ever written to your device. Both values stay in your browser: they are never transmitted to us, never sent to any third party, and we cannot read them.

Why there is no cookie banner

Under § 25 (1) TDDDG, storing information on your device normally requires your consent. § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG exempts storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you have expressly requested. Remembering a language you have just actively selected is precisely such a case: you asked for it by clicking, and the stored value is the minimum needed to deliver it.

Since there is nothing else on this site that requires consent, there is no consent to collect. The German data protection supervisory authorities explicitly advise against displaying consent banners where consent is not actually required, because such a banner creates the misleading impression that you have a choice that in reality does not exist.

What our server records

Like every website, this one is delivered by a server, and that server keeps access logs. This happens for every request, independently of cookies:

  • your IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested
  • the referring page, if any
  • browser type and version, and operating system
  • HTTP status code and amount of data transferred

This data is used solely to deliver the website reliably, to detect and defend against attacks and abuse, and to diagnose technical errors. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: we have a legitimate interest in the secure, stable and error-free operation of this website. This data is not combined with other data and is not used to analyse your behaviour. It is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for these purposes.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, which delivers the pages and provides protection against attacks. When you visit, your request necessarily passes through Cloudflare's servers, which is why your IP address is processed there. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in the secure and efficient delivery of this website. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place.

This involves a transfer of data to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and the transfer is additionally safeguarded by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Further information is available in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Links to other websites

The link to Instagram is an ordinary hyperlink, not an embedded plugin. No data is sent to Instagram or Meta while you are on this site. Only if you actively click the link does your browser connect to Instagram, where that provider's own privacy policy applies.

Removing the stored language preference

You can delete the stored value at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings (usually under “Privacy” or “Site settings”). The site will then simply open in English again the next time you visit. Using private or incognito mode also discards it when you close the window.

Was auf Ihrem Gerät gespeichert wird

NameArtZweckInhaltDauer

Beide Werte bleiben in Ihrem Browser: Sie werden nicht an uns übermittelt, nicht an Dritte weitergegeben, und wir können sie nicht auslesen.

Warum es kein Cookie-Banner gibt

Nach § 25 Abs. 1 TDDDG ist das Speichern von Informationen auf Ihrem Endgerät grundsätzlich einwilligungsbedürftig. § 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG nimmt davon Speicherungen aus, die unbedingt erforderlich sind, um einen von Ihnen ausdrücklich gewünschten Dienst bereitzustellen. Das Speichern einer soeben aktiv gewählten Sprache ist genau ein solcher Fall: Sie haben ihn durch Ihren Klick gewünscht, und der gespeicherte Wert ist das Minimum, das dafür nötig ist.

Da es auf dieser Website sonst nichts gibt, was einer Einwilligung bedürfte, gibt es auch keine Einwilligung einzuholen. Die deutschen Datenschutz­aufsichts­behörden raten ausdrücklich davon ab, Einwilligungsbanner einzusetzen, wenn eine Einwilligung gar nicht erforderlich ist – denn ein solches Banner erweckt den missverständlichen Eindruck, Sie hätten eine Wahl, die in Wirklichkeit nicht besteht.

Was unser Server protokolliert

Wie jede Website wird auch diese von einem Server ausgeliefert, der Zugriffe protokolliert. Das geschieht bei jeder Anfrage, unabhängig von Cookies:

  • Ihre IP-Adresse
  • Datum und Uhrzeit der Anfrage
  • die aufgerufene Seite oder Datei
  • die zuvor besuchte Seite (Referrer), sofern vorhanden
  • Browsertyp und -version sowie Betriebssystem
  • HTTP-Statuscode und übertragene Datenmenge

Diese Daten dienen ausschließlich der zuverlässigen Auslieferung der Website, der Erkennung und Abwehr von Angriffen und Missbrauch sowie der Diagnose technischer Fehler. Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO: Wir haben ein berechtigtes Interesse am sicheren, stabilen und fehlerfreien Betrieb dieser Website. Eine Zusammenführung mit anderen Daten oder eine Auswertung Ihres Verhaltens findet nicht statt. Die Daten werden gelöscht, sobald sie für diese Zwecke nicht mehr erforderlich sind.
